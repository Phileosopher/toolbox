## Specific Distros

Microsoft-familiar Linux:  
[Zorin](https://zorin.com/)

Stable everyday use:  
[Fedora Linux Workstation](https://fedoraproject.org/workstation/),
[Ubuntu Linux](https://ubuntu.com/)

Stable everyday use (low-spec):
[Linux Mint](https://linuxmint.com/), [Puppy Linux](https://puppylinux.com/)

Specific hardware (embedded):

[libreCMC](https://librecmc.org/) (all-in-one),
[Raspbian](https://www.raspbian.org/) ([Raspberry Pi](cs-embedded.md)),
[Steam OS](https://store.steampowered.com/steamos/) (Steam machines),
[antiX](https://antixlinux.com/) (older computers)

Emergency portable backup:  
[SystemRescue](https://www.system-rescue.org/)

Extremely small and portable:  
[Knoppix](https://www.knopper.net/knoppix/index-en.html),
[Tiny Core Linux](http://tinycorelinux.net/)

[Penetration testing](cs-infosec-pentest.md):  
[Kali Linux](https://www.kali.org/),
[Pentoo](https://pentoo.ch/)

Media center:  
[Kodi](https://kodi.tv/)

[Computer security](cs-infosec.md):  
[Qubes](https://www.qubes-os.org/) (through [virtualization](cs-distsys-vm.md)),
[Tails](https://tails.boum.org/) (through [anonymity](cs-infosec.md))

Full customizability:  
[Arch Linux](https://archlinux.org/)

GNU-honoring:  
[Debian](https://www.debian.org/),
[Devuan](https://www.devuan.org/)

There are more [enterprise](cs-distsys-enterprise.md) distros as well.
